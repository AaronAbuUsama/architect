Refund policy

Read references/policy-2026.md before refunding. Check the charge in billing first; never refund a charge that is already disputed.

  • Duplicate charge: refund the newer one in full.
  • Cancel within 14 days: prorate with scripts/prorate.ts.
  • Over $25: request approval; say why in one line.
