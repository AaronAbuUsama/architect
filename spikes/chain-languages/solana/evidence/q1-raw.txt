What disappears without Anchor (native `solana-program` or Pinocchio): no IDL => no generated surface to classify; the discriminator is whatever the author
chose (often a 1-byte enum tag), errors are a hand-written ProgramError::Custom(n) enum, and accounts/PDAs are documented only in code.
Shank (macro annotations) or Codama can emit an IDL from native programs; NOT tested here. The generator would then own the discriminator + error-number scheme itself.
