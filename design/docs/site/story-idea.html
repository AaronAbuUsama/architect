1. The idea

Part of the story. Deeper: frame/story.md, section 1.

It starts as a conversation with your agent, in chat, on a diagram, or in Figma.

you    Members should be able to spend company money without asking each time. Up to a limit, monthly.
agent  So a new thing, an allowance: one organisation, one member, a limit, what's been spent.
       Who can grant one? Only the owner?
you    Yes. And they can revoke it.
agent  States, then: active, exhausted when it's all spent, revoked. Can a revoked one come back?
you    No.
agent  What happens if a spend is more than what's left?
you    Refuse it, and say how much is left.
agent  And a phone that retries the same spend after a timeout?
you    Spends once.

What comes out is not code yet. It is three kinds of fact, each with a place in blueprint/.

From the conversationBecomes
"an allowance has a limit and what's spent"a thing, with fields
"active, exhausted, revoked; revoked is final"a machine: states and moves
"only the owner grants", "say how much is left", "spends once"operations, with roles, errors and idempotent
"the owner revokes, then the member can't spend"a journey, written as a story

A state diagram drawn in FigJam or as UML is the machine. Three boxes and three arrows are exactly states and moves. Forman can draw the machine back as the same diagram, so the picture and the code are the same thing seen twice (the docs projection, in projections.md).

Blanks

Anything you don't know yet stays a blank: Blueprint.todo("what happens at the month's end"). A blank is a question, not a gap. It is written down, it shows in the model, and it becomes a task. Chapter 3 says more.

Today and proposed

Today. Blanks exist (Blueprint.todo), and the compiler finds them. No tool turns a conversation or a diagram into a blueprint: the agent writes the files by hand.

Proposed. The planning agent writes straight into blueprint/ through the builder's bp.write (builder.md), and forman check answers in the same turn. A FigJam or Mermaid state diagram is read as a machine, and drawn back from one.
